# Week 1: "The War on The Hand" (Episodes 1–7)

Episodes 1 and 2 are animated in `episodes/ep1.py` and `episodes/ep2.py`. Episodes 3–7 are scripted below with timed beats.

## Ep 3: The Outside (~40s)

Dill lands in the giant kitchen, narrowly avoids becoming a sandwich topping, and races home, only to get locked out.

| Time | What we see | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Title card: "Episode 3: The Outside" | Fanfare, slightly off-key |
| 2–6s | The Hand drops Dill onto a plate on a huge kitchen counter. Everything is enormous. | Echoey whoosh; awe choir |
| 6–11s | A giant bread slice lands next to him, then lettuce, then tomato. Dill realizes he's next. | Thump ×3; gasp |
| 11–14s | The top slice of bread descends like a ceiling closing in. | Rising slide whistle; tension drums |
| 14–18s | Dill rolls off the plate and slides down a spoon across the counter (callback to Ep 1). | Zip; spoon scrape |
| 18–23s | He bounces off a sugar bowl, skids through sprinkles, somersaults onto the floor in a heroic pose. | Ping, swish, crunch, ta-da |
| 23–29s | Dill sprints to the fridge; its door is slowly swinging shut. | Running blips; ticking clock |
| 29–34s | He leaps for the gap; the door slams as he arrives. He smacks flat against it and slowly peels off. | BONK; slow peel; slide whistle down |
| 34–37s | Inside, in the dark, Crumb wears the crown. Three knocks sound on the door; Crumb's eyes go huge. | Three muffled knocks |
| 37–40s | TO BE CONTINUED... | Duck squeak |

Duck: top shelf, now at the front edge. New assets: kitchen counter, plate, bread, lettuce, tomato, sugar bowl, sprinkles, fridge exterior.

## Ep 4: Knock Knock (~42s)

Crumb thinks the knocking is The Hand and builds an egg barricade, while Dill climbs the fridge magnets to get back in.

| Time | What we see | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Title card: "Episode 4: Knock Knock" | Fanfare |
| 2–7s | Split screen. Inside: Crumb jumps at every knock. Outside: Dill pounds on the door, red-faced. | Knock, scream, knock, scream |
| 7–13s | Crumb stacks eggs into a wobbly wall, hides behind it with a toothpick spear. | Egg clonks; nervous gibberish |
| 13–19s | Dill climbs fridge magnets like a ladder (banana, star, pizza; the pizza slides down with him). He climbs again. | Grunts; magnet thunk; sad trombone; drum |
| 19–23s | Dill hangs from the handle, pulling with all his might. Nothing. | Straining gibberish; creak |
| 23–27s | The Hand opens the door at that exact moment; Dill swings in on it like a wrecking ball. | Choir; whoosh |
| 27–31s | Dill crashes through the egg wall. Both are covered in yolk. | Crack ×3; gooey splat |
| 31–35s | They hug, dripping. Crumb hands back the crown; Dill puts it on with great dignity. | Heartwarming chord; drips |
| 35–39s | Door closes. Dill turns to his throne spot: the Duck is sitting exactly there. | Silence; slow zoom; low cello |
| 39–42s | TO BE CONTINUED... | Duck squeak |

Duck: moved to the bottom shelf throne spot (also visible for one frame behind the egg wall). New assets: split screen, eggs and yolk, magnets, handle.

## Ep 5: The Usurper (~40s)

Dill tries everything to remove the Duck from his throne spot. The Duck does not move.

| Time | What we see | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Title card: "Episode 5: The Usurper" | Fanfare in a minor key |
| 2–6s | The Duck now wears a bottle-cap crown. Dill, still yolky, points at it, trembling. | Steam hiss; furious gibberish |
| 6–11s | Staring contest: Dill's eyes water, big eye twitches; he blinks first and falls over. | Tense violin; blink; thud |
| 11–16s | Dance-off: Dill flails wildly. The Duck does nothing. | Funky bass; "hoo!"; crickets |
| 16–21s | Dill charges, bounces off the Duck like a trampoline into the milk carton. | Running; BOING; crash |
| 21–26s | Crumb breaks off a corner of himself as a gift, winces, bows. | Crunch; tiny "ow"; chime |
| 26–29s | The Duck squeaks. Once. | Silence, then one sharp squeak |
| 29–33s | Dill and Crumb scream, run in circles, dive behind the jello. | Double scream; boing |
| 33–37s | Top shelf: the Pickle Jar lid starts turning by itself, click by click. | Click... click...; low drone |
| 37–40s | TO BE CONTINUED... | Duck squeak |

Duck: on the throne spot; its crown tilts after the squeak. New assets: bottle-cap crown, Dill dance, jar lid turning.

## Ep 6: The Jar Opens (~42s)

Dill's pickle relatives climb out and bow to the Duck instead of him. Dill is exiled to the crisper drawer.

| Time | What we see | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Title card: "Episode 6: The Jar Opens" | Fanfare with ominous organ |
| 2–7s | The lid pops off; little pickles march down in a line. Last: tiny, ancient Grandma Gherkin with a cane. | Pop; marching drum; cane taps |
| 7–11s | Dill beams and opens his arms, waiting for their bow. | Triumphant swell |
| 11–15s | They walk past him and bow to the Duck. | Record scratch; bowing whooshes ×5 |
| 15–20s | Dill rallies them with a speech and sword; Grandma Gherkin bonks him with her cane. | Gibberish; tiny bonk; slide whistle down |
| 20–26s | The pickles carry Dill like a parade float and dump him in the crisper drawer. Crumb waves a tiny goodbye. | Sad march; drawer "shhhk-thunk" |
| 26–31s | In the dim drawer, a wilted lettuce leaf pats Dill's back. | Sad violin; wind |
| 31–36s | Dill finds a doodle scratched on the wall: the Hand holding the Duck, with hearts. | Discovery sting; slow zoom |
| 36–39s | Dill's eyes go wide; he grips the wall. | DUN DUN DUNNN |
| 39–42s | TO BE CONTINUED... | Duck squeak |

Duck: throne spot, slow nod at Grandma Gherkin's bow. New assets: 5 small pickles, Grandma Gherkin, crisper interior, wilted lettuce, doodle.

## Ep 7: The Truth About the Duck (~45s)

Dill tricks the Duck into squeaking in front of The Hand, which takes the Duck away for bath time. Dill gets his kingdom back, but something new arrives.

| Time | What we see | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Title card: "Episode 7: The Truth About the Duck" | Heroic fanfare |
| 2–7s | Dill escapes the drawer with the lettuce's help and acts out the doodle for Crumb. | Creak; whispered gibberish |
| 7–12s | The plan: Crumb makes the Duck squeak exactly when the door opens. Crumb gulps, salutes. | Spy music; salute "ting" |
| 12–17s | Crumb tiptoes past the bowing pickles, freezing whenever one looks. | Tiptoe plinks; freeze ticks |
| 17–20s | Dill gives a thumbs up from the door seal. The door opens. | Choir |
| 20–23s | Crumb jumps on the Duck: a huge squeak right in front of The Hand. | HUGE echoing squeak |
| 23–28s | The Hand gently picks up the Duck and carries it away. Faintly: a bathtub filling. The pickles gasp. | Gasps; running water; squeaks fading |
| 28–33s | The Hand returns the (half-eaten) butter dish to its spot. Door closes. | Clunk; thud |
| 33–38s | The pickles bow to Dill. He climbs his throne, crown on, sword raised. Crumb cries happy tears. | Fanfare; confetti pops |
| 38–42s | Celebration in the dark... the camera pans to a single egg on the top shelf. It wobbles. A crack. | Music stops; crack |
| 42–45s | SEASON 2 COMING SOON... | Tiny "peep"; eerie sting |

Duck: taken away; brief silhouette in bathtub steam. New assets: door-seal hiding spot, confetti, cracking egg.
