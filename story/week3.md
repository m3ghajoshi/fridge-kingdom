# Week 3: "Two Ducks" + "The Ice King" (Episodes 22–35)

Two linked 7-episode arcs. Arc A (Eps 22–28) continues straight from Ep 21's cliffhanger: the frozen Duck in the ice block blinks and the ice cracks. It wakes up, and it is nothing like the Duck. Arc B (Eps 29–35) pays off the Week 2 plant: the Ice King finally comes down through the vent, and he isn't what anyone expected.

**New characters this week**

- **Squeak** (Ep 22): the second rubber duck, thawed out of the ice block. Design: the same rubber-duck shape as the Duck but frost-bleached pale butter-cream (#FFF3BF body, #FFE8A3 wing), a small chipped notch in the tip of its orange beak, a pale-blue frost rim along its tail tip, big round eyes with an icy-blue glint, and the same orange-red crimped bottle-cap crown tilted the OTHER way (to the left). No towel turban. Personality: the Duck's exact opposite: never stops moving, bouncy, nosy, delighted by everything. Running gag: squeaks at the worst possible moment, and every squeak summons the Hand's footsteps.
- **The Ice King** (shadow since Ep 11; first full appearance Ep 29): a huge, ancient blue-raspberry twin ice pop: two fused rounded blue bars (#4DABF7 / #1C7ED6, frosty white bevels), two wooden sticks for legs, a long beard of hanging icicles, bushy frost eyebrows, the icy-blue slit-pupil eyes seen behind the vent, a jagged icicle crown, and a ragged cape of white freezer-frost. Personality: booming and grumpy on the outside, deeply lonely on the inside. Running gag: everything he touches freezes, including the things he hugs.

**Threads paid off this week:** how the Duck moved around in the dark (Ep 4): there were two Ducks, and Squeak used to slide down the vent at night and swap places (Ep 25); how Grandma knows the freezer and what her cane taps said (Ep 13): she and the Ice King have been tap-pen-pals for years (Ep 30); the Ice King's identity (Eps 29–31); Dill's lost crown (Grandma has worn it since Ep 9): Dill gets a crown back in Ep 35.
**New mystery planted (pays off Week 4+):** Wilt's tally marks on the crisper mural have been counting down to something: the last tally (Ep 35) sits next to a scratched drawing of the Hand holding a giant sponge and a trash bag. The Big Clean-Out is coming.
**Side characters' bigger moments:** Peep finally meets someone who copies HIM (Ep 26); Chip saves everyone in the Big Chill (Ep 27) and becomes the vent's ambassador (Ep 34); Grandma Gherkin's secret past (Ep 30).
