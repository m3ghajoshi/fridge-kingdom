# Week 2: "The Egg" + "The Frozen North" (Episodes 8–21)

Two linked 7-episode arcs. Arc A (Eps 8–14) continues straight from Ep 7's cliffhanger: the egg on the top shelf hatches. Arc B (Eps 15–21) follows a mystery planted in Arc A: something lives above, in the freezer.

**New characters this week**

- **Peep** (Ep 8): a tiny chick that hatches from the top-shelf egg. Design: round pale-lemon fluffy body (paler and fuzzier than the Duck, scalloped fluff outline), three-feather tuft on top, huge glossy black eyes with white shines, tiny orange triangle beak, stubby wings, orange twig feet, and the top half of its eggshell worn as a tilted helmet (white, zig-zag rim). Personality: fearless, chaotic, always hungry, adores Crumb. Running gag: copies whatever the last character did, in miniature (and usually better).
- **Chip** (Ep 15): a small ice cube who escapes the freezer through the ceiling vent. Design: pale-blue translucent cube with rounded corners, white frost highlights, a trapped air-bubble "freckle," shy little eyes, a wobbly mouth, and a constant puff of frosty breath. Personality: shy, polite, homesick and scared of whatever rules the freezer. Running gag: melts a little whenever someone is nice to him (drip, drip), and Crumb mops him up and puts him back together.

**Threads paid off this week:** who scratched the doodle in the crisper drawer (Wilt did, Ep 11); the Duck's bath-time departure (it returns, changed, Ep 14); how the Duck moved around in the dark (Ep 19 hints: there may be more than one Duck).
**New mysteries planted:** tapping from the ceiling vent and the shadowy "Ice King" of the freezer (pays off Week 3); a second Duck frozen in an ice block (Ep 19 → Ep 21 cliffhanger).
**Side character's bigger moment:** Wilt (Ep 11), Grandma Gherkin (Ep 13), Peep (Ep 20).
